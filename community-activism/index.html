More Than a Bar

Giving Back to Joliet

At Double "J" Sports Bar, we believe a great bar is part of a great community. We're proud to support the causes and organizations that make Joliet a better place to live.

What We Support

Our Initiatives

Health & Awareness
Pat Clark Cancer Foundation

Double "J" proudly supports the Pat Clark Cancer Foundation, helping raise awareness and funds for cancer research and support services right here in the Joliet community.

Annual Event
Double "J" Annual Golf Outing

Our annual golf outing brings the Joliet community together for a great day on the course while raising money for local causes. Stay tuned for this year's date and registration details.
