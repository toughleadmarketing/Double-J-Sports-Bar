More Than a Bar

Giving Back to Joliet

At Double "J" Sports Bar, we believe a great bar is part of a great community. We're proud to support the causes and organizations that make Joliet a better place to live.

What We Support

Our Initiatives

Health & Awareness
Pat Clark Cancer Foundation

Double "J" proudly supports the Pat Clark Cancer Foundation, helping raise awareness and funds for cancer research and support services in the Joliet community.

Community Giving
Toys for Tots & Food Drives

Every holiday season, Double "J" serves as a drop-off location and active organizer for Toys for Tots drives and local food drives — making sure no family in Joliet goes without.

Youth & Local Sports
Team Sponsorships

We sponsor local youth and adult sports teams throughout the Joliet area. From jerseys to tournament fees, Double "J" is in your corner both on and off the field.

Annual Event
Double "J" Annual Golf Outing

Our annual golf outing brings the community together for a great day on the course while raising money for local causes. Stay tuned for this year's date and registration details.

Kuira Bá Kitchen

We're proud to welcome Kuira Bá Kitchen — a new carryout restaurant connected directly to Double "J". Enjoy fresh, homemade Mexican food made daily right here at the bar. Outside food and delivery are also always welcome!
